## Results

Official results for the paper accepted at **IEEE QCE26**:
*Strengthening security and noise resistance in one-way quantum key distribution protocols through hypercube-based quantum walks*, D. Polzoni, T. Bianchi, M. Conti.

- `c_analysis/`: overlap parameter $c$ and optimal $t$, circle and hypercube ([main_c_circle.py](../c_analysis/main_c_circle.py), [main_c_hypercube.py](../c_analysis/main_c_hypercube.py))
- `optimized_parameters/`: grid-search optima over $(P, F, \phi, \theta, t)$ ([main_opt_param_c.py](../optimized_parameters/main_opt_param_c.py)) and QER-optimal settings ([main_opt_param_qer_dn.py](../qer_analysis/main_opt_param_qer_dn.py), [main_opt_param_qer_dmp.py](../qer_analysis/main_opt_param_qer_dmp.py))
- `qer_analysis/`: QER sweeps under depolarizing and amplitude/phase-damping noise ([main_dn.py](../qer_analysis/main_dn.py), [main_dmp.py](../qer_analysis/main_dmp.py))
- `plots/`: figures generated by [plot_scripts/](../plot_scripts/)
